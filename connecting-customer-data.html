Connecting customer data across channels

A simple model for one customer view without a rip-and-replace.

Connecting customer data across channelsCX · 7 min read
CX · 7 min read

One customer view does not require replacing every system. It requires a clear plan for identity, integration and use.

Key takeaways

01

Start from the questions

Pick the three things your teams most need to know about a customer.

02

Unify identity first

Match records reliably before you try to analyze anything.

03

Integrate in layers

Connect systems gradually instead of attempting a big-bang rebuild.

04

Put insight where people work

A dashboard nobody opens helps no one. Surface it inside the agent's screen.

05

Govern consent

Respect preferences and regulations from the start.
