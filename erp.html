AI-Native ERP

The core that thinks, acts and improves.

Traditional ERP records what happened. AI-native ERP helps decide what happens next. Most enterprises add AI on top of their ERP as a separate layer. ARRON builds intelligence into the core itself, so processes can sense, decide and act, with people kept in control.

What AI-native ERP delivers

Autonomous Processes

AI agents run routine work across finance, supply chain, procurement and order-to-cash, from matching and reconciling to replenishing and resolving exceptions.

Predictive Operations

Forecasting and early-warning signals for demand, cash, inventory and supply risk, so the business acts before issues hit.

Conversational ERP

People ask, approve and act in natural language instead of moving between screens and transactions.

Intelligent Close & Control

Continuous reconciliation, anomaly detection and automated compliance checks for a faster, cleaner close.

Connected Data Foundation

One trusted data layer across ERP and the wider enterprise, which gives AI the context it needs to reason and act.
